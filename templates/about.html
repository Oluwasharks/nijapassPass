About NijaPass

How We Started

NijaPass was born in Abuja in late 2025 from a simple, frustrating experience. Our founder, Joseph Ogunsakin, was hosting a friend visiting Nigeria for the first time from Canada. What should have been an exciting trip turned into a logistics nightmare. His friend's foreign bank card was declined at a hotel in Wuse, the car hire driver quoted three different prices in one day, and finding a good restaurant that was safe and tourist-friendly required calling five different people. That night, Joseph realized: Nigeria is one of the most beautiful, vibrant countries in the world, with breathtaking locations like Zuma Rock, Obudu, Yankari, and Lekki, but there is no single trusted platform for a first-time visitor.

We looked at the numbers. Nigeria receives over 2 million visitors yearly for business, tourism, and diaspora homecoming. Yet 78% report being overcharged for basic services. Existing booking platforms are global and don't understand local realities: they don't include trusted drivers, they don't know which hotels have stable power, and they don't solve the biggest pain - payments. Foreign cards fail, POS fails, and carrying cash is unsafe.

What NijaPass Does Today

NijaPass is Nigeria's first travel guide and wallet built specifically for first-time visitors. We are not just another booking site. We are a wallet-first ecosystem. You fund your NijaPass wallet once with Naira, via Paystack, bank transfer, or even crypto to Naira conversion, and then you can pay for everything: vetted 4 and 5-star hotels in Abuja and Lagos like Transcorp Hilton and Eko Hotels, car hire with verified drivers who speak English and know the city, curated restaurants like NOK and JeVinik that meet international hygiene standards, and private jet hire for business travelers who need to move from Lagos to Abuja in 55 minutes.

Every hotel, car, and restaurant you see on NijaPass has been physically verified by our small team in Garki, Abuja. We take real photos, not stock. We negotiate real fixed prices in Naira, so you are not charged tourist prices. And our wallet protects you - you only release payment after service is confirmed.

Where We Are Heading

Our vision is bold: to become the official digital pass for anyone entering Nigeria. By 2027, we want to integrate with immigration at the airport - you land, you open NijaPass, and everything is ready: your eSIM, your hotel, your driver waiting with your name. We are building partnerships with the Federal Ministry of Tourism to include official tourism sites, with private security companies for safe travel, and with local communities in Obudu and Yankari to ensure tourism money reaches them.

We are starting as a demo in Abuja and Lagos, but we will expand to Port Harcourt, Calabar, Kano, and Enugu. We will add features like a panic button that shares your live location with our support team, a language guide that translates Pidgin, and an AI tour guide that can explain the history of Zuma Rock as you drive past it. We are green and white because we believe in Nigeria. Nigeria is not just a destination; it is an experience. And NijaPass is your key to experiencing it safely, affordably, and memorably. Welcome to Nigeria. Welcome to NijaPass.
